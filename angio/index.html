        Why it is empty

        A 3D reconstruction here would mean vessels segmented from one real patient's angiographic volume (3D rotational angiography, CTA or MRA), shown as that patient's anatomy. No openly licensed volume that covers both carotid systems, the ECA branches and the vertebrobasilar system could be obtained for this build.

        The nearest candidate checked, OpenNeuro ds005096 (CC0), is time-of-flight MRA of intracranial aneurysms: it shows the circle of Willis but not the ECA or neck, and its segmentations cover the aneurysm and parent artery only. A reconstruction from it would need a full vessel segmentation of one subject, kept separate from every other subject and from the schematic model.

        The 3D view in the Simulated injection tab is the schematic teaching model, not a reconstruction.
