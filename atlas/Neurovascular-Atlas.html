Cranial motorAmb

Nucleus ambiguus

Deep lateral reticular formation dorsal to olive

What this structure does

Branchial motor control of pharynx and larynx via IX and X.

If injured

Left lesion: Left palatal and vocal-fold weakness, dysphagia and hoarseness; uvula may pull away from the weak side.

Crossing rule

Predominantly ipsilateral peripheral motor output; supranuclear input is bilateral.

Course, blood supply & pitfalls

Course

Lateral medullary reticular formation → exiting glossopharyngeal and vagal motor fibers.

Supply associated with this level

  • Vertebral / PICA medullary branches

Branch contributions overlap. This list identifies plausible supply, not a proven occlusion.

Localization pitfall

Gag reflex is variable in healthy people; do not localize from an absent gag alone.

UBC Functional Neuroanatomy ↗

Read this level

Medial lesions can unite the pyramid, medial lemniscus and hypoglossal fascicles. Lateral lesions can unite spinal V, anterolateral fibers, nucleus ambiguus, vestibular nuclei, inferior peduncle and descending sympathetic fibers.
