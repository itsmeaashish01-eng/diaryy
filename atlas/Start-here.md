# Neurovascular Atlas

Open **Neurovascular-Atlas.html** in a modern browser. It works offline without installation or a server. Keep **Neurovascular-Atlas.pdf** and **Stroke-Localization-Notes.pdf** in the same folder so the app's download links work.

1. Choose an anatomical level in the left sidebar.
2. Set lesion side and language dominance.
3. Select a numbered marker to inspect a structure, then add it to the lesion simulation. You can also check several structures below the map.
4. Load a vascular example to see a related combination of structures and manifestations. Changing levels clears the current lesion.
5. Use Pathways & fibers for full courses, crossings, locations and arterial supply. The reference book and external source links are available in their own views.
6. Open **3D neuraxis** to see the fifteen plates standing in space. Drag to rotate, wheel or pinch to zoom, click a marker to inspect it. A structure marked on several plates is drawn as a course through them, and the named decussations are drawn crossing the midline. Side, dominance, the lesion simulation and the vascular examples work as they do in the lesion explorer. The map panel in the lesion explorer also has a 2D / 3D switch that shows the current plate among its neighbours.

The reference includes 97 mapped structures, 14 additional pathways, 15 locator maps, 26 vascular/syndrome patterns, 19 chapters and an illustrated PDF with linked contents and a structure index.

**Stroke-Localization-Notes.pdf** is the concise, 10-page study companion: crossing rules, three medullary levels, three pontine levels, three midbrain levels, thalamus and capsule, cortical regions, visual and cerebellar pathways, advanced fibers, vascular patterns, and worked cases. **Stroke-Localization-Notes.md** contains the same notes in an editable text format. Download the notes from the app's Reference book view or open the PDF directly.

These are educational schematics. Markers are approximate positions, not anatomical boundaries, patient MRI coordinates or exact infarct volumes. The 3D view is a schematic montage of the same plates: relative plate sizes, spacing and marker positions are teaching approximations, and the line drawn between two plates is interpolated, not measured. The simulator is not a validated diagnostic model. Exact lesion extent requires clinical imaging and interpretation.

Online publishing remains pending; these files run locally. External reference links require internet access.
