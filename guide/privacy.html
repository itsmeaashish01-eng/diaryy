Privacy

Last updated 16 September 2026

RoamGuide has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. There is nowhere for your data to be collected to, which is the short version of this entire page.

Your location

The app asks for your location only when you open the Nearby tab and switch it on. It is used to work out what is around you, how far away each thing is and which way.

Your position is held in memory while the app is open. It is not stored, not written into your trips, and not sent to us — there is no us to send it to.

One exception, and it is the whole of it. To answer "what is around me", the app sends a single coordinate to Wikipedia, which replies with what it has articles about nearby. Nothing else accompanies that request: no identifier, no account, no history. Wikipedia's own privacy policy governs what they do with the request — see wikimediafoundation.org.

Map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap as you pan the map, which necessarily tells their servers roughly which part of the world you are looking at, exactly as any map does.

What is stored, and where

Your trips, saved places, places you have marked as visited, your own pins and your settings are stored on the device, in the app's own storage. They never leave it.

Deleting the app deletes them. There is no backup on our side and no way for us to recover them, which is why the app has an Export button — use it before a trip you would mind losing.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or email address.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • Nothing sold or shared, because nothing is collected.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Changes, and getting in touch

If this ever changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top moves.
