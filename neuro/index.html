What's cool in neurology

The field is moving fast. Here's the proof.

Short reads on the discoveries that changed what neurologists can do, with the paper so you can check it yourself.

Brain–computer interfaces

Implanted electrodes now decode attempted speech

In 2023, two teams decoded speech from people with ALS and brainstem stroke at roughly 60–78 words per minute, using cortical arrays and machine learning. Speech that was lost can be partly restored.

Willett et al.; Metzger et al., Nature 2023

Sleep

The brain rinses itself while you sleep

Interstitial space widens during sleep and CSF flow clears metabolites such as amyloid-β faster: the glymphatic system.

Xie et al., Science 2013

Dementia

A blood test for Alzheimer pathology

Plasma p-tau217 tracks amyloid and tau pathology closely enough to change how patients reach diagnosis.

Palmqvist et al., JAMA 2024

Genetics

Spinal muscular atrophy, rewritten

Antisense oligonucleotides and gene replacement turned a fatal infant disease into one where many children sit and walk.

Finkel et al.; Mendell et al., NEJM 2017

Movement

Tremor treated without an incision

MR-guided focused ultrasound lesions the VIM thalamus through an intact skull for essential tremor.

Elias et al., NEJM 2016

Quantum Stroke

Stroke, from the artery to the outcome

Neuroanatomy you can localise with, the trials that moved practice, and the code-stroke workflow minute by minute.

Summaries are for learning. Read the full paper and your local protocol before applying any result.

  1. 0 min

    Door

    Stroke alert on arrival. Glucose at the bedside, last known well documented.

  2. ≤10 min

    Doctor at bedside

    ABCs, NIHSS, weight, anticoagulant history.

  3. ≤15 min

    Stroke team

    Neurology on scene, family located for history.

  4. ≤25 min

    CT started

    Non-contrast CT plus CT angiography from arch to vertex.

  5. ≤45 min

    Imaging read

    Bleed excluded, ASPECTS scored, large-vessel occlusion called.

  6. ≤60 min

    Needle

    Thrombolysis if eligible. Many centres aim for 45.

  7. ≤90 min

    Groin puncture

    Thrombectomy for LVO, without waiting to see a lytic response.
